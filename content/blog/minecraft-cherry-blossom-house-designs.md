Cherry blossom biomes gave Minecraft its prettiest palette: pink leaves, pale wood, and soft light. A house built in that palette feels like a vacation base — and because the whole look runs on a handful of blocks, it is also one of the easiest styles to build well. These five designs range from a 10-minute cottage to a pavilion that anchors an entire garden.

## The Cherry Blossom Palette

Every design in this guide uses the same five blocks, so memorize them once:

- **Walls:** cherry planks with white terracotta trim. The pale wood does the heavy lifting.
- **Roof:** cherry slabs or deepslate tiles for contrast.
- **Leaves:** cherry leaves in every overhang and around the foundation.
- **Accents:** pink terracotta, pink wool, and flowering azalea bushes.
- **Light:** lanterns on cherry fence posts — never torches, which read orange against the pink.

## 1. The Hillside Cottage

The fastest cherry house: a 9×7 rectangle on a gentle slope, with a single room and a 2-block-wide porch. Key details are the roof — cherry slabs sloped 2 deep with deepslate tiles at the ridge — and leaf clusters tucked under every roof corner. Add a flowering azalea at the door. Build time: about 20 minutes, roughly 700 blocks.

## 2. The L-Shaped Garden House

For a bigger footprint without a big look, build an L-shape: a 13×9 main room with a 7×7 bedroom wing. The interior corner of the L gets a 5-block circle of white concrete with a cherry tree planted in the center — the tree visually holds the whole house together. Windows go on the outer walls only, which keeps the house cozy and the garden private.

## 3. The Round Blossom Pavilion

This is the design that surprises people: a **15-block circle** of cherry planks with a ring of pillars on the outside edge. Build the base ring with the [circle generator](/circle/15/) blueprint, raise the wall 3 blocks, and leave the center open to the sky. The roof is a 2-block-wide ring of cherry slabs around the pillars, open in the middle so the tree planted inside grows through the hole. At night the lanterns on the pillars turn it into the best screenshot spot on the server.

## 4. The Waterfront Retreat

Place a 15×11 house on a small pond's edge and wrap the foundation in cherry leaves. The pond is a 21×13 oval — see our [oval guide](/blog/how-to-build-an-oval-in-minecraft/) for the outline — with a 2-block bridge of cherry planks leading to the door. The pink-and-white palette against the blue water is the whole design; there is no other decoration needed. Add an underwater sea lantern under the bridge for the evening glow.

## 5. The Palace Pavilion

For the showpiece: a central 21-block circle with four 9-block wings at the cardinal points. Each wing gets its own small roof, the center gets a taller, tiered roof in deepslate, and every roofline is lined with cherry leaves and lanterns. The garden around it is a circle of cherry trees planted at 5-block spacing — roughly 12 trees in a 33-block ring. This is a two-afternoon build, but every block is a repeat of the cottage's details.

## Gardens and Paths That Sell the Look

The house is half the build; the garden is the other half. Three plantings that make cherry houses read instantly:

- **Ring planting.** A ring of cherry trees at 4–5 block spacing around the house. Harvest the wood as you build so the canopy fills in evenly.
- **Petal paths.** White concrete powder paths with pink and white flowers scattered along the edges. No straight paths — curves only.
- **Pink lighting.** Pink candles in the windows and lanterns at the gate. The palette stays pink even after sunset.
