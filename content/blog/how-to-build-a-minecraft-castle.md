Every Minecraft player dreams of a castle, and almost every castle gets abandoned halfway through the walls. The fix is planning: a castle is a grid of repeating parts — walls, towers, gatehouse, keep — and once you decide the sizes of those parts, the build becomes a checklist. This guide walks through a 51×51 castle with four corner towers, built in a weekend.

## The Plan: Decide Before You Dig

Castle math is simple. Our castle is a square, 51 blocks wide per side, with:

- **Curtain walls** 3 blocks thick and 8 blocks tall between the towers.
- **Corner towers** 15 blocks wide, rising 5 blocks above the walls.
- **A gatehouse** on the south wall, 21 blocks wide, with a portcullis gap.
- **A keep** in the center, 25×25 with a 2-block setback at height 12.

Write those numbers down before placing a single block. The single biggest castle-killer is building walls first and discovering the towers don't fit the corners.

## Walls and Corner Towers

**Step 1: Mark the footprint.** Place corner markers at a 51×51 square, then outline the whole perimeter with stone blocks. This outline is the outer face of your walls.

**Step 2: Build the curtain walls.** Fill the wall lines 3 blocks thick and 8 blocks high with stone. Add a crenellation band on top: one full block, one gap, across the entire length. The crenellation is what makes a wall read as a castle wall from a distance, so don't skip it — 3-block-thick walls let you walk the battlements while mobs wander the ground floor.

**Step 3: Build the corner towers.** At each corner, build a **15-block circular tower** — the [circle blueprint](/circle/15/) gives you the ring. Towers are 13 blocks tall, 5 above the wall line, with a single door at ground level facing inward. Inside, a spiral staircase climbs the inside curve; the top gets a ring of crenellation and two windows facing outward.

**Step 4: Connect the walkways.** On the battlements, cut a 2-block-wide walkway strip along the inner edge of each wall. Doors at each tower's wall-facing side connect the towers into one continuous defensive loop.

## The Gatehouse and Keep

**The gatehouse:** the south wall gets a 21-block-wide, 12-block-tall structure instead of plain wall. The entrance is a 3-block-wide, 5-block-tall arch in the center; a drawbridge of spruce planks extends 4 blocks over a dry moat. Flank the arch with two 9-block mini-towers and add a second portcullis arch on the inner side. The space between the two arches is the perfect place for an iron door and a kill chamber.

**The keep:** in the center of the courtyard, build a 25×25 keep. Walls rise 12 blocks, then step back 2 blocks on all sides for the upper story. The keep is the interior build of the castle — throne room on the ground floor (25×25 minus stairs), storage in the cellar, and a bedroom in the upper story. A 9-block circular tower on the keep's roof holds the flag.

## Courtyard, Moat, and Light

The courtyard between walls and keep is where the castle feels alive:

- **Well and garden.** A 9-block circular well (our [fountain ideas](/blog/best-minecraft-fountain-designs/) work perfectly here) and a 15-block circle of farmland for food.
- **Moat.** Dig the ground outside the walls 2 blocks deep and fill with water. A 51×51 castle with a 3-wide moat needs about 900 water blocks — fill it with sources, not streams, and light the moat floor with sea lanterns every 8 blocks.
- **Lighting.** Torches on the battlements every 4 blocks, sea lanterns in every tower stairwell. Castles are huge and dark; light every interior room as you finish it.

## Block Counts and Scaling

Rough budgets for the weekend castle:

| Part | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| 4 walls, 3 thick, 8 tall + crenellation | 5,600 |
| 4 corner towers (15-block circles) | 3,200 |
| Gatehouse | 1,200 |
| Keep | 4,500 |
| Courtyard floor and extras | 1,500 |

That is about 16,000 blocks — a big but very doable weekend with a friend. For a smaller project, scale every number down by one third: 33×33 walls, 11-block towers, and a 15-block keep. The proportions hold at any size, which is the whole trick of castle building.

## Start with the Footprint

Castles fail when builders wing the layout. Grab the [circle generator](/?t=circle&d=15) for the tower rings and well, lay the full 51×51 footprint in outline blocks first, and walk the perimeter before building. When the outline looks right, the rest is just walls, towers, and time.
